# Journal

## Where the journal actually lives

**In Notion, not in this file.** Alif chose the Notion Journal database on 2026-08-10, captured on his phone.

> Journal database: https://app.notion.com/p/a5f8d48c3fed4fb59a10b75d42a93f1b

This file is the mirror and the reference. The exact daily steps are in `CLAUDE.md` so the habit has one path and is not re-decided every morning.

## The five minutes

1. Open the Journal database in the Notion app on your phone. Favourite it once so it is on the home tab and you never navigate to it again.
2. Tap **New**.
3. Type one line in **Note**: something that actually happened today. Not a topic. A moment.
4. Set **Date** to today. Leave **State** as `raw`.
5. If there is more, put it in **Detail**: who else was there, what was actually said, what happened next. If there is not, stop. One line is a complete entry.

That is it. Five minutes, and most days two.

## What counts as an entry

A moment, a decision or a mistake. Something with a when and a where.

Good: "Client asked in the Thursday review why the buttons had moved. Nobody in the call owned the component. I said I'd find out, which was the honest answer and also the one that made me look worst."

Not an entry: "Thinking about design systems governance."

The second one is a topic. Topics produce posts that could have been written by anyone. Moments produce posts that could only have been written by Alif, which is the entire point of the system.

If a day genuinely had nothing, write "nothing today". That is real data and it is better than a manufactured entry.

## Pipeline states

Every entry moves through these. **You never have to change the state yourself.** DRIP moves them.

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `raw` | Just captured. Everything starts here. |
| `beat` | DRIP has identified this as usable material and added it to the backlog in `data/story.md`. |
| `drafted` | A draft has been written from it. |
| `queued` | The draft is in the Notion Content Queue with a date against it. |
| `posted` | Alif copied it out and published it. |
| `logged` | The numbers have been pulled into the Post Log by ANALYZE. |

A beat that reaches `posted` is struck from the backlog in `data/story.md` and never used again.

## The rule that protects this

**If the journal has not been updated in a week, DRIP says so and does not draft.**

This is the most important line in the file. An empty week is information: it means nothing happened worth writing about, or that the habit slipped. Both are fine and both are recoverable.

What is not recoverable is a system that fills the gap by inventing material. That produces plausible, sourceless posts, and the audience Alif is trying to reach is exactly the audience that can tell. Inventing material is worse than an empty week.

## If capture keeps slipping

Do not add a reminder app or a second tool. The failure is almost never the tool.

Two things to try, in order: attach it to something already daily (the commute home, closing the laptop), and drop the bar to one line. Five words in the Note field with nothing in Detail is a valid entry and always has been.

If it is still slipping after two weeks, that is worth saying out loud in a DRIP session. The cadence may be wrong, not the habit.
