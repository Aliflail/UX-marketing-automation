## How to read the score

Per the Kickstart:

- **4 to 6 correct** — around chance. The voice files are holding. Start publishing.
- **7 to 9 correct** — the machine ones are showing. Ask which passage felt wrong first and which word did it, fix that one thing in the voice file, run it again.
- **10 correct** — the voice files describe a genre rather than a person. Rebuild with far more of Alif's actual sentences and far fewer adjectives about him.

## The caveat that matters

Alif's five passages are **transcribed speech**, lightly cleaned of "um", "uh" and repeated words. He has almost no published writing, so there were no real posts to use. Everything else in them is word for word.

The machine five are written as posts.

**So a high score is ambiguous.** The reader may have spotted his voice, or may simply have spotted that half the passages sound like a person talking. Only the first is a finding about the voice files. If the score comes back at 8 or above, ask each reader *what* gave it away before concluding anything, and discount any answer that amounts to "those ones read like speech".

This ambiguity disappears once Alif has published a handful of real posts. Those become the corpus, and the next run of this test uses them.

The blood and toe story from round 4. It is his best-told piece of material and the most obviously his. Family members are in it, and family is behind the fence in `data/rules.md`. Using it here would have made the test easier and the rails weaker.
