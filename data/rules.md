# Privacy rails

Rebuilt from round 5 of the interview, 2026-08-10. Source: `data/corpus/interview.md`.

Where a rail is in Alif's own words, his words are printed. Where a rail is a conservative default he has not spoken to, it is marked `[default]` and can only be loosened by him.

---

## Clients and teammates

**His words, and the firmest rail in the file:**

> "Nothing about my clients I would never write about because it's against my, um, you know, I've signed it in IBM. So nothing about my actual clients or my teammates names. I wouldn't write anything about them."

This is a signed obligation, not a preference. It does not bend for a good post.

- No client named, ever. Not the current one, not past ones, not the ones already on a CV.
- No teammate named. Not managers, not creative directors, not peers, not the ones he admires. He has warm things to say about the creative directors in the Dubai studio; those get written as behaviour, never attached to a name.
- No detail that functions as a name. "The creative director on the retail account" identifies someone to everyone in the building.
- Client work is described by shape and problem only. "A client project where the budget was too low for the number of iterations they wanted" is fine. Naming the account is not.
- No screenshots of client work. Not blurred, not redacted, not from an early version. `[default]`
- Nothing under NDA, and when unsure whether something is, assume it is. `[default]`
- Nothing about a pitch, bid or account that has not been announced. `[default]`
- He does not speak for IBM iX. Posts must not read as company position. `[default]`

## Tone, and never being negative about people

**His words:**

> "I will not curse anyone. I will not be negative. I am always nice, and I don't, uh, be too critiquing anybody."

This is a rail, not just a style note, because a post that puts someone down is the clearest possible signal that he did not write it.

- No punching down. Not at juniors, not at clients, not at other designers' work.
- No subtweets, no vaguebooking about something at work.
- Criticise practices, never people. He does this naturally: *"they just replay stuff, which is, like, a hell a lot of work, which is the worst. I hate that."* The heat is on the practice and no person is in the sentence.
- Nothing posted while angry about something that happened that day. If it is still true tomorrow it is a better post. `[default]`

## The research fence

**His words, given when asked what he would hate to see quoted back:**

> "Like user research is key because I'm always an adviser of it. I don't want it to be quoted against."

He advocates for research constantly, and works in conditions where it gets cut, including on his current project. A post that states "user research is key" as a clean rule becomes exactly the quote he is describing, and the first person to point that out will be right.

**Rule: any post about research holds both halves.** The advocacy, and the fact that he has shipped without it. Never the rule on its own.

## Money

`[default]` Not raised in the interview. Held tight until he says otherwise.

- No salary, his or anyone's.
- No day rates, project values or contract sizes. Note he has already discussed a client budget being low in general terms; that is fine. Numbers are not.
- No Ommelo revenue, funding, valuation or burn.
- No user or waitlist numbers unless he deliberately decides to publish one.
- No pricing under consideration.

## Anything under negotiation

`[default]`

- No job conversations, his or anyone else's.
- No promotion, review or internal role discussion.
- No partnership, client or acquisition talk before it is announced.
- No unreleased Ommelo plans beyond what he has already said publicly.

## Claims

`[default]`, and enforced in the drafting prompt.

- Never invent a number, result, metric or outcome.
- Never invent a user, a client quote or a testimonial.
- No experience he has not actually had. If a draft needs a story he does not have, the draft is wrong, not the story.
- No implied credential.
- If a claim cannot be backed up when someone in the comments asks for the detail, it does not go in.

## Internal tooling

`[default]` He mentioned an IBM internal AI tool by name in the interview. **That does not go in a post.** Internal tooling, internal process and internal org detail are not public, and naming an unreleased internal system is the kind of thing that is nobody's idea of a good week.

Public tools he uses personally are fine.

## The screenshot check

`[default]` Before any image goes out: tabs, sidebar, notifications, file names, the browser history strip, the calendar in the corner, and any reflection. This is where confidential detail actually escapes, not in the copy.
