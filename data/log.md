# Log

## Where the log lives

**In Notion.**

> Post Log: https://app.notion.com/p/eb7976ee86a44916a449f93c13ff1c4e

Empty. It fills as things get published and ANALYZE pulls the numbers in.

## Columns

| Column | Type | What goes in it |
|---|---|---|
| **Post** | Title | The hook, so the row is recognisable at a glance. |
| **Date** | Date | Date and time actually published, not the date it was queued. |
| **Channel** | Select | LinkedIn, X, Reddit, Product Hunt. |
| **Format** | Select | One of the seven named formats. |
| **Subject** | Select | One of the four recurring subjects. |
| **Media** | Select | None, Screenshot, Image, Carousel, Video. |
| **Ending type** | Select | Genuine question, Observation, Flat statement, No ending. Never a call to action. |
| **Impressions** | Number | Pulled from the platform. Never from memory. |
| **Out of network percent** | Number | Share of reach beyond existing followers. |
| **Comments from strangers** | Number | Comments from people Alif does not already know. |
| **Mature** | Checkbox | Ticked once the post is more than 24 hours old. |
| **What I think happened** | Text | One line, in Alif's words, written before looking for a pattern. |

## The maturity rule

**Anything under twenty-four hours old is marked immature and excluded from every comparison.**

Unticked `Mature` rows do not appear in any ANALYZE calculation. This is not a formality. Early numbers are dominated by who happened to be online in the first hour, and a post compared at hour three against one compared at day five will always look worse regardless of quality. Comparing immature posts is the fastest way to learn something false and then act on it for a month.

## Why these three numbers and not the obvious ones

**Out-of-network percent** matters more than impressions. Impressions mostly measure how many people already follow you; out-of-network measures whether the platform decided to show it to anyone new. That is the only number that indicates growth rather than existing reach.

**Comments from strangers** is the only engagement number worth much. Likes are close to noise, and comments from people you already know are a measure of your friendships. A stranger writing a paragraph is worth more than two hundred likes, particularly when the goal is reaching design leads who do not know Alif yet.

**Impressions** is here for context and denominator, not as a score.

Followers are deliberately not a column. Nothing in this system is optimised for follower count.

## The written line

`What I think happened` is filled in by Alif, in his own words, **before** looking at whether it fits a pattern. It is written first for a reason: once you have seen the numbers across ten posts you start explaining each one in terms of the pattern, and the independent read is gone.

Over a few months this column is the most valuable one in the database, because it is a record of his judgement that can be checked against outcomes.
